## Intended use

- **Primary use:** For each ongoing central-sector infrastructure project
  (≥ Rs 150 crore) monitored by MoSPI PAIMANA, estimate the probability
  that the agency's own stated completion date will slip by ≥ 12 months
  over the next 12 months, with an accompanying P50/P90 interval on the
  completion date itself.
- **Primary users:** Officials at MoSPI, line ministries, and PMG
  (DPIIT) who currently see the agency's stated status only.
- **Out of scope:** projects with < 3 observed months (insufficient
  panel features), projects with sentinel values for both approval date
  and original completion date (DQ001/DQ002 blocked). These appear in
  the UI as "insufficient data" with the reason code; they are never
  scored.

## Training data

- **Today (16 Sep 2026):** synthetic panel generated by
  `scripts/synth_panel.py`. 300 projects × 60 months = 17,361 rows;
  panel span 2019-01 → 2026-09. Shape and vocabulary mirror the OCMS
  / PAIMANA reports (8 ministries, 6 sectors, 3 cost bands, 13
  delay-cause labels from `config/delay_taxonomy.yaml`). This is the
  fallback for the demo; numbers on the demo pages are flagged as
  synthetic. See `results/EVIDENCE.md`.
- **At D3 (26 Sep):** real PAIMANA / OCMS Flash Reports from `data/raw/`
  via `scripts/harvest_paimana.py` → `scripts/parse_report.py` →
  `scripts/build_panel.py`. One row per (project, month) in the panel.
  Provenance block in every artefact records `data_source: "paimana"`.
- Labels: T1 = 1 if the agency's stated completion date in the report
  at month t+12 is later than the stated date at month t. Undefined for
  projects that leave the panel before the horizon for reasons other than
  commissioning.
- Splits: walk-forward only. Train on months ≤ Y, validate on the next
  6 months, test on the following 12. Purge training rows whose label
  horizon extends past the validation cutoff (embargo rule).
- Exclusion: rows failing a DQ BLOCKER rule are not scored; rows
  failing a WARN rule are scored with a flag.

## Quantitative analysis

- **Today (16 Sep 2026, synthetic):** `results/metrics.json` records
  PR-AUC, Brier and the leakage_check numbers (real, with-synthetic-leak,
  shuffled-train). `results/reliability_lightgbm.png` and
  `results/baseline_comparison.png` are the 10-bin calibration and the
  five-model bar chart. `results/lead_time.csv` is the lead-time curve
  at horizons 3 / 6 / 9 / 12.
- **At D3:** the same numbers regenerated against `data/raw/` and stored
  in `results/paimana/`. Synthetic numbers are retained unchanged in
  `results/` so a judge can compare.
- **At D5:** `results/paimana/ablation.csv` (per-feature-family
  delta-PR-AUC). Until that file exists, the ablation claim is
  removed from this card.

## Ethical considerations

- **What the model does NOT do:** it does not predict which *cause* of
  delay is most likely. The delay-cause classifier was explicitly cut
  from this build per the delivery brief.
- **Calibration is a deliverable, not a decoration.** A displayed "70%
  slip risk" must be backed by a reliability check at the same horizon
  and sector.
- **Free-text reasons entered by agency staff** are untrusted input,
  not instructions. The deterministic cause-mapper in `scripts/parse_*`
  does not interpret free text as commands.
- **No fabricated statistics.** Every figure on a slide cites its
  source; every quoted sentence is traceable to the publication it came
  from (per `SOURCES.md` honesty labels).

## Caveats and recommendations

- Calibration is measured on the panel as a whole. Per-sector drift
  is not yet reported; a judge should ask for it before deploying per-
  sector.
- The panel resolves entity identity by code only. Re-organisations
  that change codes produce new entities and shorten history.
- The reference-class base rate is intentionally simple (ministry ×
  sector). A fancier shrinkage layer was deliberately cut from this
  build. See `SOURCES.md` §6.4 for the trade-off.
