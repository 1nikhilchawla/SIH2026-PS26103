Leakage guard (G3)

Cost-up model (G5) - honest weak result

Verdict: we do not ship a monthly cost-overrun forecast. 9 walk-forward folds were run; only 1 carries enough positive cases (≥30) to say anything at all.

The best score belongs to ministry_base_rate. A trivial baseline wins, so the machine-learning model adds nothing here and we say so.

Why - and why we did not tune our way out of it

The obvious fix is to lower the threshold that defines an overrun. We tested it. Dropping it from 0.500% to 0% moves the positive count from 114 to 136 - it does not unlock the target.

The constraint is the corpus, not the threshold: 98.55% of project-months carry exactly zero change in revised cost, only 200 projects ever change it at all, and 88 of 136 changes land in the single month 2026-06. Revised cost in these reports is a near-static administrative field revised in batches, not a monthly signal. Forecasting it month-by-month is the wrong question to ask of this data.

Reproduce: python scripts/diagnose_cost_target.py → results/paimana/cost_target_diagnostic.json

Per the brief's rule - a weak result honestly reported is worth more than a tuned one - this page states the negative result rather than hiding it behind the slip model, which does work.
